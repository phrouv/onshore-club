# 🧍 Persona Template

## 1. **Goals and Motivations**
- **Main Goals**: (What they want to achieve)
- **Motivations**: (What drives their decisions)

## 2. **Challenges and Pain Points**
- **Frustrations**: (What causes stress or dissatisfaction)
- **Barriers**: (What prevents them from achieving goals)

## 3. **Needs and Expectations**
- **Product/Service Needs**: (What they expect from your solution)
- **Support Needs**: (Preferred communication channels, help expectations)

## 4. **Quotes**
- A few representative quotes that capture their mindset or attitude.

## 5. **Scenario or Story**
- A short narrative describing a day in their life or a situation where they interact with your product/service.

## 6. **Nice to have**
- A short narrative describing additional features or services that would enhance their experience but are not critical.
